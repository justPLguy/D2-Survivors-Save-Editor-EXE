## Why we moved off the `.exe`

The downloadable **D2 Survivors editor** kept running into the same two problems: people had to **download and install** something just to tweak a save, and antivirus programs kept throwing **false-positive virus warnings** at a tool that was never doing anything harmful. That is a terrible first impression, and plenty of players bounced off it before even trying the editor.

So we did what players were actually asking for: **we moved the whole thing into the browser.** No installer, no `.exe`, nothing for your antivirus to panic about — you just open a link and the **D2 Survivors save editor** runs. And since it is a web page now, it works the same on **PC and on your phone**, for free.
